When a boiler problem is an emergency

A boiler with no heat in winter, a leak spreading from the unit or a smell of gas all count. Check that the thermostat is calling for heat, that the power switch on the unit is on and that the pressure gauge reads in the normal range printed on it.

If you smell gas, leave and call from outside. Make sure carbon monoxide alarms are working, since a faulty boiler is a classic source. For no hot water rather than no heat, see water heater repair.

What to do before the plumber arrives

  • Confirm the thermostat is set to heat and above room temperature.
  • Check the power switch and the circuit breaker.
  • Look at the pressure gauge and compare it with the label.
  • Test your carbon monoxide alarms and open a window if one sounds.

Maintenance that prevents no-heat calls

  • Have the boiler serviced once a year before the cold season.
  • Bleed radiators if they are cold at the top.
  • Keep the area around the boiler clear of storage.
  • Check the pressure gauge monthly in winter.
  • Test carbon monoxide alarms when you change the clocks.

A boiler that is serviced early rarely fails in January. A boiler that is never serviced often does, and parts and appointments are scarce during a cold snap.

Hot water and steam are different systems

A hot water boiler pushes heated water through pipes to radiators or baseboards. A steam boiler boils water and sends steam up to radiators, then the steam condenses and returns. The repairs differ, since steam systems care about water level and venting and hot water systems care about pressure and circulation.

Boilers in Rockville homes

We treat no-heat boiler calls as emergency work because heat is a safety need in winter. Hot water or steam boilers are most common in older houses, where radiators or baseboard heat survive from earlier decades.

Older systems are simple and repairable, but parts for very old units can be scarce. What this means for you is that an honest assessment should cover the repair, the remaining life and whether replacing the boiler now beats a third repair in a winter. See water heater replacement if a combined unit supplies your hot water, and our Gaithersburg page if you are north of the city.

Where this comes from. According to Montgomery County's permit guidance, plumbing permits are handled through WSSC Water, which has its own Plumbing and Fuel Gas Code. Check with WSSC Water for what your job needs, because rules change. Gas piping to a boiler counts as fuel gas work.
