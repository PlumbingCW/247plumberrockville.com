Before a Plumber Arrives

A few minutes of preparation lets the plumber start work the moment they walk in.

Find the main

Know where the main shutoff valve is and be ready to close it. It is usually where the water line enters near the meter.

Clear the path

Move items away from the problem area and the utility room so the plumber can reach pipes.

Stop the use

Stop running water, laundry and dishwashers if a drain is backing up.

Note the details

Write down when it started and what you see, and take photos for your insurer.

How Gaithersburg grew, and what that means for pipes

Gaithersburg was incorporated in 1878 and became a city in 1968. For most of its history it stayed largely rural, and then it grew fast, from 8,344 residents in 1970 to 26,424 in 1980. Today it has more than 69,000 residents and a Shady Grove Metro station on the edge of Rockville.

The practical meaning is that homes of that age may have had their water heaters replaced already and may have supply and drain lines old enough to deserve a look. A camera on a sewer lateral or a pressure test on a supply line tells you more than guessing. Our leak detection service covers the hidden failures.

Common plumbing problems in Gaithersburg homes

  • Slow main drains and repeat clogs, handled with sewer line cleaning.
  • Water heaters from the 1990s and early 2000s that are leaking or failing.
  • Basement moisture after heavy rain, where a sump pump matters.
  • Sudden pressure drops, which may point to a service line problem.

Plumbing at different ages of Gaithersburg housing

Gaithersburg's housing runs from older homes in the original town to townhomes and condos built in the decades since the 1970s growth. The oldest houses can still have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains. Newer homes use different materials and have different issues, such as fixture and valve wear.
