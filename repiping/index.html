Signs your pipes are at the end

Galvanized steel pipe corrodes from the inside and narrows over time. You notice low pressure at some fixtures, rusty or brown water after the house sits, and leaks that appear at joints. Copper can also develop pinhole leaks, especially where water chemistry or installation was poor.

One leak is a repair. Several in a year, in different places, are a pattern. Our burst pipe repair page explains emergency fixes, and leak detection finds the source of an unexplained wet spot.

How a repipe is planned

We start with a walkthrough to count fixtures and look at how the old pipe runs. Then we plan the new route, usually through basements, closets and attic spaces, to keep wall openings small. Call to ask about availability and timing.

Afterward, we pressure test the new system before closing walls, so any leak is found while it is easy to fix. Patching drywall and paint are separate steps, and we help you plan them.

Living through the work

Repiping is easier than most people fear. We protect floors, set up dust control and keep the water on for part of each day. You can use the house, though you may want to plan around a day or two without showers. We lay out the schedule before we begin, so you know what to expect each day.

Copper or PEX

Both can last decades when installed well. PEX is flexible, goes through walls with fewer cuts and tolerates a freeze better. Copper has a long record and resists heat and UV. We recommend based on your house, your budget and how much drywall you are willing to open.

Older Rockville pipes

Galvanized supply pipe was used in some older houses, and a few may still have it. By contrast, King Farm and Fallsgrove homes use newer materials. The City of Rockville says no lead service lines have turned up in its inventory, and a limited number of galvanized service lines remain, so the house-side pipe is where older systems usually show their age.

A good plan sequences the work so you keep water most of the day. If the street-side line is also failing, doing it at the same visit saves a second excavation, as described on water service line repair. Our North Bethesda page covers homes of the same era.

Where this comes from. According to the City of Rockville's 2025 water quality report, lead tested below 1 part per billion at the 90th percentile, and the report lists no hardness figure. The City's service line inventory is the place to ask about your service line material.
