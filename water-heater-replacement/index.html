When a failed water heater is an emergency

A slow drip at the base of a tank is a warning. A steady stream is an emergency. Once the steel tank corrodes through, water follows the floor drain or the nearest low spot, and a 40 to 50 gallon tank can empty in the time it takes to find the shutoff valve.

No hot water in January is its own kind of urgent, especially with children or older relatives in the home. If you are unsure whether the problem is a repair or a full replacement, our page on water heater repair explains how we tell the two apart.

Do this before the plumber arrives

  • Switch the breaker off for an electric heater, or turn the gas control to off for a gas heater.
  • Close the cold water supply valve on the pipe entering the top of the tank.
  • Open one hot tap upstairs to relieve pressure, and move stored items off the floor around the heater.
  • If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not flip switches.

Repair or replace: how to decide

Tank water heaters wear out from the inside. Sediment settles, the anode rod is consumed, and the steel starts to rust. As a rough guide, tanks start to fail in the range of 8 to 12 years, and the date stamped on the serial label tells you where yours stands.

Questions to settle before the new unit goes in

A good replacement starts with a few answers. How many people use hot water at the same time in the morning, where will the discharge pipe run, and is the gas line or electric circuit sized for the new model? Settling these first avoids a second visit and a surprise on the invoice.

  • Do you want the same fuel type or a change from gas to electric?
  • Is there a floor drain near the heater, or does the pan need a drain line?
  • Are the shutoff valves on the water lines old and stiff?
  • Do you plan to sell soon, in which case a documented new unit helps?

What replacement day looks like in a Rockville home

We drain and disconnect the old unit, set the new one on a proper drain pan, and reconnect water, gas or electric, and the relief valve with discharge piping. Then we fill the tank, purge air, light or power it, and check combustion and venting on gas models before we leave.
