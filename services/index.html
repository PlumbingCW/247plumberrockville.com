How to choose between services

Start with the symptom and not the part. A gurgling toilet and a bubbling basement drain point to a drain or sewer problem, a damp wall points to leak detection and a cold shower points to a water heater. Once we know where the problem sits, we recommend the smallest repair that will hold.

We would rather tell you a small fix will do than sell a big job. If a pipe is near the end of its life, we say so plainly and explain the options, from a spot repair to full repiping. For emergencies, read the first section of the service page that fits, since each one explains what to do before the plumber arrives.
