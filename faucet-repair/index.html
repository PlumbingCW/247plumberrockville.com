When a faucet problem is an emergency

A dripping faucet is a slow loss. A faucet with a cracked body, a failed supply hose or a stuck valve that will not shut off is a flood waiting to happen. Outdoor spigots that burst in freezing weather also release water inside the wall.

If water is spraying, shut the local valve first. If the valve itself leaks, shut the main and call us. For a drain that is slow instead, our drain cleaning page is the one to read.

What to do right now

  • Turn the two small valves under the sink clockwise.
  • If they leak or stick, close the main water valve.
  • Put a bucket or towels under the leak.
  • Do not force corroded handles, which can snap the stem.

Repair or replace the faucet

A cartridge or washer swap restores most dripping faucets for a modest cost. Replace the faucet if the body is cracked, if corrosion has eaten the threads or if parts are no longer made. A new faucet also gives you the chance to fit new supply lines and valves in one visit.

If you are choosing a replacement, buy a model with a standard cartridge that plumbers can service for years. Rare designs look nice but become hard to repair.

Outdoor faucets and winter

A frost-proof hose bib has a long stem that puts the shutoff inside the warm house wall. If a hose is left connected in winter, water can freeze in the stem and split the pipe behind the wall, which only shows when spring brings the leak. Disconnect hoses in autumn and shut the inside valve if your spigot has one.

Keep a small kit on hand: an adjustable wrench, plumber's tape and a spare aerator. They handle the quick fixes and make the next call shorter.

Faucets and Rockville water

Rockville treats much of its own water from the Potomac River and WSSC Water supplies the rest. Water chemistry affects how fast aerators clog and cartridges wear, but the city's report does not list a hardness figure, so we do not guess. If you notice white crust on fixtures, a yearly cleaning of aerators and a test of your tap give you the facts.

Older shutoff valves under sinks can corrode and fail when turned. Replacing them during a faucet repair is cheap insurance. If the faucet is part of a bigger project, see plumbing installation and toilet repair. Our Aspen Hill page covers nearby homes.

Where this comes from. According to the City of Rockville's 2025 water quality report, lead tested below 1 part per billion at the 90th percentile, and the report lists no hardness figure.
