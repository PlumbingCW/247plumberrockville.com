How a tankless heater differs from a tank

A tank keeps 40 to 50 gallons hot all day. A tankless unit heats water as it flows through a heat exchanger, so there is no standby tank and no running out until demand exceeds the unit's flow rating.

That flow limit is the catch. If two showers and a dishwasher run at once, a unit sized too small gives you lukewarm water. We calculate the demand before recommending a model, and if a standard tank suits you better, our page on water heater installation covers that route.

What owners wish they had known

Many people expect unlimited hot water and then meet the flow limit when two showers and a dishwasher run together. Others are surprised that the unit needs yearly descaling, and that a power cut means no hot water even with gas available.

None of this makes tankless a bad choice. It means the right choice comes from your usage, your gas supply and your willingness to maintain it, and we talk through all three before you buy.

Common tankless error codes

Error codes vary by brand, but the causes fall into a few groups: ignition failure, exhaust or intake blockage, a flow too low to fire the burner and overheating from scale. Ask what to expect before work begins.

Care and troubleshooting

Scale is the main enemy. Mineral buildup narrows the heat exchanger and triggers error codes, and a yearly vinegar or descaling flush resets that. We do not quote a hardness number for Rockville because the city's report does not list one, so a test of your own tap is the honest way to know.

A cold-water sandwich, where a burst of cold water slips between hot draws, is normal behavior for many units and not a fault. Persistent error codes, ignition failures or fan noise are worth a service call. For urgent failures see water heater repair.

Tankless in Rockville homes

In a house with a small utility closet, a wall-mounted unit can free space for storage. In King Farm townhomes the gas meter and run length decide the answer. What this means for you is that the same unit can be easy in one house and costly in the next, so we look before we quote. Our Derwood page covers nearby homes with similar layouts.
