Before a Plumber Arrives

Condos, apartments and houses each need slightly different prep.

Shutoff first

Locate the shutoff for your unit or the main for your house and be ready to close it.

Tell the manager

In a condo or apartment, call building management so water to the stack can be shut if needed.

Clear access

Open cabinets under sinks and clear the closet around the water heater.

Protect belongings

Lift items off the floor and photograph any water damage.
